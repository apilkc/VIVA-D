How it works

  1. Add evidence — upload a photo, video, or document from your device, or paste a public Facebook / X / Twitter post link.
  2. Set the location — drop a pin on the map at the exact spot. Not sure? Check "I don't know exact location" and the system places it along the river.
  3. Add details — who took it, when, and what it shows. Photos with GPS data fill the location automatically.
  4. It's published — the item appears on the map instantly, archived permanently in cloud storage.

Note: Items are published immediately and cannot be deleted. All information you enter is shown publicly for verification. Social imports only work for public, accessible posts.

About this archive

Virtual Imagery and Video Archive for Disasters (VIVA-D) is an open collection of geotagged photographs and videos documenting the impacts of natural disasters, with an initial focus on floods and landslides. It organizes visual data and associated metadata to support disaster reconnaissance, research, assessment, and resilience efforts. This Rasuwa archive is part of that work and was created by Apil KC.
