How it works

  1. Choose one source. Upload a file from your device (photo, video, or document), or paste a public Facebook, X, or Twitter post URL.
  2. Archive it. A local file is sent to the project's Google Cloud Storage bucket. A supported public social URL is downloaded first, then sent to that same bucket.
  3. Set the location. Drop a pin on the mini-map at the exact place where the photo or video was taken — or let the system place it approximately along the river if you're not sure.
  4. Describe it. Tell us who took it, when, and what it shows. Tick the confirmation box to state the item is authentic.
  5. It appears instantly. Visitors see the archived media on the map. When available, the original social post remains linked as provenance.

Important about social posts

  • Imports work only for public posts that the downloader can access without login. Private, deleted, restricted, or unsupported posts will fail.
  • Do not use this to bypass platform access controls. Only archive media you are allowed to copy and share.
  • Items are published immediately and cannot be deleted.
  • Anything you enter — including your contact details — is shown publicly to help people verify the evidence.
